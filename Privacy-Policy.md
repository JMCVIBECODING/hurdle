## 1. What we collect
- **Email address** — when you lock an entry or join our updates.
- **Game data** — your picks, results, scores, the codename shown on the leaderboard, and the date/time you played.
- **Marketing preference** — whether you opted in to receive emails.
- **Technical & usage data** — IP address, device/browser type, and pages viewed, collected via cookies and similar technologies (see section 6).

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

## 2. How we use it and our legal bases
- **To run the Games and the prize competition** — store your entry, score it, rank the leaderboard, and contact and verify winners. *Legal basis: performance of a contract and our legitimate interests in operating the Games.*
- **To send you marketing/updates** (the daily NAP and game updates) — *only if you opt in. Legal basis: consent, which you can withdraw at any time.*
- **To measure and improve our advertising and the Games** — analytics and conversion measurement. *Legal basis: consent (for non-essential cookies) and our legitimate interests.*
- **To prevent fraud and abuse and to comply with law** — *legal basis: legitimate interests and legal obligation.*

You are never required to consent to marketing to enter or win.

## 3. Who we share it with (our processors)
We use trusted service providers who process data on our behalf:
- **Supabase** — database hosting (stores entries, picks, emails).
- **Beehiiv** — email newsletter platform (for opted-in subscribers).
- **Resend** — transactional email (entry confirmation emails).
- **Vercel** — website hosting.
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram)** — advertising and conversion measurement via the Meta Pixel and Conversions API (see section 6).

We do not sell your personal data.

## 4. International transfers
Some providers (e.g. Resend, Meta) process data outside the UK/EEA. Where they do, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the UK International Data Transfer Agreement or Standard Contractual Clauses.

## 5. How long we keep it
- **Entrants and winners:** for the duration of the event period and a reasonable period afterwards to administer prizes, verification, and our records.
- **Marketing subscribers:** until you unsubscribe.
- **Analytics data:** in line with the providers' standard retention periods.

## 6. Cookies and tracking
We use cookies and similar technologies, including the **Meta Pixel and Conversions API**, to measure our advertising and understand site usage. Non-essential cookies are only used where you have given consent via our cookie banner, and you can decline or change your choice at any time. The Meta Pixel may match a hashed version of your email to your Meta account for advertising measurement.

## 7. Your rights
Under UK GDPR you have the right to: access your data; correct it; delete it; restrict or object to processing; data portability; and withdraw consent at any time. To exercise any of these, email **privacy@hurdlegame.app**.

You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

## 8. Marketing opt-out
Every marketing email includes an unsubscribe link. You can opt out at any time, and doing so will not affect your competition entry.

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date above shows the latest version.
